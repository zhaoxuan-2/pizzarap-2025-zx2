Introduction

In the Philippines, there is a growing need for healthy and reasonably priced food options, particularly among consumers who are health conscious. Pizzarap! offers a unique twist on the traditional pizza roll by adding tasty and nutritious alternatives. Our product line includes Creamy Moringa, Cheesy Tuna, and Chicken Pastil — offering a healthier yet delectable take on typical pizza rolls.

Project Summary

"Pizzarap! Franchising Corp" comes from Pizza + Sarap (Filipino for "delicious"). Objective: create and market creative pizza rolls that provide affordable and healthy alternatives.

Location: Bulacan Provincial Capitol View Park, Malolos City — near busy streets, business districts, schools, and families.

Target Market Segments

A

Kids (6-12 years old)

Fun, healthy snack option

Children are drawn to snacks that are flavorful, visually appealing, and easy to eat. Parents prioritize nutritious and safe options. Our Creamy Moringa pizza roll, enriched with malunggay, provides a fun and delicious alternative to unhealthy junk food.

B

Teens & Adults (13-30)

Trendy, filling, budget-friendly

Students juggle academics, activities, and social lives — they need convenient, grab-and-go food. Our Cheesy Tuna and Chicken Pastil flavors offer the perfect balance of affordability, portability, and indulgence for fast-paced lifestyles.

C

Teachers & School Staff

Quick, nutritious, non-heavy meals

Educators have demanding schedules with limited time for sit-down meals. They need something substantial but not too heavy to stay energized. Our pizza rolls provide an ideal balance of protein, carbohydrates, and essential nutrients.

D

Employees & Office Workers

Mess-free, satisfying snacks

Office professionals need food that's delicious but convenient to consume without creating a mess. Our pizza rolls are compact, satisfying, and won't disrupt workflow — perfect for short breaks or eating at desks.

Geographic Segmentation

Located at Bulacan Provincial Capitol View Park. High foot traffic near schools, offices, and public markets. Online ordering expands reach.

Psychographic Strategy

Appeals to those who value health, convenience, and affordability. Health-conscious eaters, food enthusiasts, parents, and socially responsible consumers.

Pricing Strategy

Cost-plus pricing with 40% markup on production cost. Ensures profitability while maintaining competitive and reasonable prices.

Channel of Distribution

Direct-to-customer through physical store, online sales via social media, and delivery service for convenience and wider reach.

Promotion & Advertising

Facebook, TikTok, Instagram campaigns. Free samples in schools. Colorful posters. Fun packaging. Special promos and offers.

Competitive Advantage

Healthier ingredients, affordable pricing, and local sustainable sourcing differentiate Pizzarap from traditional fast-food competitors.

Provides Employment

Hires production workers, delivery riders, cashiers, and marketing assistants. Benefits local farmers and poultry growers through locally sourced materials. Provides food safety training to improve employability.

Tax Compliance

Registered with DTI and BIR. Files monthly percentage tax and quarterly/annual income tax returns. Pays local taxes. Taxes fund public projects like roads, schools, and hospitals in Bulacan.

Improves Capital Base

Injects steady income flow into Malolos. Regular purchases support multiple micro-economies. Rent and tax payments boost real estate and public funds. Salaries generate consumer spending.

People Empowerment

Staff can advance from kitchen assistants to supervisors. Supports working students with flexible schedules. Buys from small farmers, empowering rural families. Women hired equally, promoting gender equality.

Creates Healthy Competition

Operates fairly by focusing on product quality, reasonable pricing, and ethical marketing. Introduces new Filipino-inspired flavors challenging old fast-food menus. Open to collaborating with local schools and food fairs. Sets an example of eco-friendly packaging and local sourcing, encouraging other businesses to adopt greener methods. Avoids false advertising and bad-mouthing rivals — customers appreciate honesty.
